Algorithmic trading books: research, execution and systems

An algorithmic trading system turns instructions into repeatable decisions and orders. Different texts explore finding an idea, evaluating data, designing execution and managing the system after deployment.

Strategy research

Algorithmic Trading considers the rationale behind momentum and mean-reversion ideas. Be explicit about the market, sampling period, costs and the limits of a backtest.

Market access and execution

Algorithmic Trading and DMA and Trading and Exchanges focus on how strategies interact with real market structure. An idea and the ability to execute it are separate questions.

Advanced methods

Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading and Algorithmic Trading Methods take a more mathematical route. Check the background a text expects before starting.
