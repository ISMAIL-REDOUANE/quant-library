Trading psychology books: decision-making and discipline

Trading psychology books explore how beliefs, uncertainty, discipline and losses can affect decisions. They offer perspectives on process rather than a substitute for position sizing, realistic costs or tested strategies.

A focused psychology text

Trading in the Zone concentrates on confidence, discipline and beliefs. Reflect on decision habits while keeping an explicit plan for risk.

Learn from interviews

Market Wizards and The New Market Wizards present traders’ different experiences. One trader’s account is not a guarantee for another.

Read stories in context

Reminiscences of a Stock Operator and Trade Like Jesse Livermore offer historical perspectives. Treat anecdotes as questions, not proof that a strategy will work today.
