Trading risk management books and reading resources

Risk management connects a trading idea to the size and combination of positions a trader holds. Books approach this from individual trades, systematic forecasts or portfolio construction.

Position sizing and systems

Systematic Trading discusses forecasts and position sizing. Advanced Futures Trading Strategies considers futures strategies and tactics; futures and leverage can magnify losses.

Portfolio perspective

Quantitative Portfolio Management considers portfolio-level questions. Correlations and concentration can matter even when individual trades look reasonable.

Risk is broader than a metric

Review liquidity, execution costs, data quality, drawdowns and model assumptions. Historical numbers alone cannot guarantee future outcomes.
